## Problem Title
SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Problem Creater's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Theme
Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## Problem Description
Expected Solution: Create a digital marketplace that:

• Connects farmers/FPOs directly with consumers and bulk buyers.
• Provides logistics support.
• Uses AI for demand forecasting and route optimization.

Benefits:

• Better prices for farmers.
• Lower prices for consumers.
• Reduced supply chain inefficiencies.

## Proposed Solution

MY-KISAN:
A unified web and mobile platform that bridges the gap between farmers, Farmer Producer Organizations,local consumers and also bulk buyers such as restaurants , supermarkets.
It bypasses the intermediaries by removing traditional multi layered supply chain , farmers retain a higher share of the consumer rupee , moreover even the consumer benefits as the price will not be inflated due to middlemen.
Integration of immutable price-discovery mechanisms and digital contracts to ensure reliable transactions, immediate digital payments, and elimination of payment delays.

## Technical Approach
FRONTEND:
React.js (cross-platform mobile app for farmers and buyers with multilingual support including regional languages support and also with customer guidance 
BACKEND:
Node.js with Express.js / Python (Django/FastAPI) to handle robust data processing and API endpoints.
DATABASE:
 PostgreSQL (for transactional data, user profiles, and catalog management) combined with Redis for real-time inventory caching.
 ROUTE OPTIMIZATION:
 Algorithms (such as Genetic Algorithms or Google OR-Tools) integrated with GIS mapping for efficient logistics scheduling, minimizing transit time and post-harvest spoilage for perishable produce.

## Feasibility and Viability
Monetization via a low-percentage transaction fee or subscription tier for bulk enterprise buyers.
Potential Challenges & Risks:
Digital Literacy: Farmers may face hurdles adopting a digital app.
Logistics in Remote Areas: First-mile pickup from rural farmgates can be fragmented.

TO OVERCOME THESE CHALLENGES:
Deploying voice-based navigation, vernacular UI/UX, and local FPO coordinators to assist farmers with onboarding and listing produce.
Partnering with existing third-party logistics (3PL) providers and rural micro-logistics fleets for reliable cold-chain and standard transport

## Impact and Benefits
Social Impact: Empowers farmers with financial independence, strengthens rural FPOs, and builds a transparent, equitable food distribution system.
Economic Impact: Increases farmer income margins by 20–30% while reducing retail purchase costs for urban consumers.
Environmental Impact: Minimizes food waste through accurate demand forecasting and reduces carbon footprint via optimized transport routing.
